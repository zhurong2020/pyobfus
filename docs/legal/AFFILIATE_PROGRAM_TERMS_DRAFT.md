# Affiliate Program Terms — Draft

**DRAFT — NOT IN EFFECT.** The pyobfus affiliate programme has not launched.
This file is a review draft, not an offer, enrolment page or promise to pay.
Final terms require maintainer approval and accounting/legal review before any
affiliate is invited.

## Proposed pilot terms

1. Participation is invitation-only and may be accepted, declined, suspended
   or ended by pyobfus at its discretion.
2. The pilot covers attributable purchases of the USD 45 pyobfus Professional
   Edition one-time licence. Community downloads, trials, donations, taxes and
   unrelated products are not commissionable.
3. The proposed commission is 20% of eligible product revenue actually
   collected, excluding tax and reduced by refunds, disputes, chargebacks and
   fraudulent or test transactions.
4. Commission remains pending for 30 days after successful payment. Approved
   balances are reviewed monthly; the proposed minimum payout is USD 50 or its
   agreed equivalent, with smaller balances carried forward.
5. Self-referrals, affiliate-controlled purchases, duplicate orders, licence
   sharing, artificial traffic, forced clicks, cookie stuffing, code injection,
   coupon leakage and misleading attribution are ineligible.
6. Affiliates must clearly disclose that they may earn a commission, close to
   each endorsement or link and in the language used for the endorsement.
7. Claims must be honest and supported by current public documentation.
   Affiliates may not claim pyobfus is impossible to reverse, guaranteed to
   secure code, endorsed by an unrelated platform, or capable of features it
   does not provide.
8. Affiliates may not impersonate pyobfus; register confusing domains/accounts;
   distribute licence keys; or bid on project, maintainer or confusingly similar
   branded search terms without written permission.
9. A copied valid referral link may receive attribution, but pyobfus may review
   identity overlap, abnormal velocity, refunds and other fraud indicators.
   Every rejection or reversal must have an internal audit reason.
10. Refunds and disputes reverse the corresponding commission in full or in
    proportion to the refunded product amount. If already paid, the adjustment
    may be carried against future balances or resolved separately.
11. Affiliates are independent parties, not employees, agents, resellers or
    representatives authorized to bind pyobfus. No sublicence or right to sell
    Pro licence keys is granted.
12. Affiliates are responsible for supplying accurate payout and legally
    required tax information and for their own tax obligations. Payout may be
    held until required records are complete.
13. Buyer personal data and licence keys are never supplied to affiliates.
    Affiliate reports use aggregate/order-reference information needed to
    explain commission decisions.
14. Programme terms and rates may change prospectively with notice. Historical
    commission records retain the rate snapshotted at the eligible purchase.
15. The pilot may be ended after the evidence review. Valid approved balances
    remain payable subject to these terms; no future sales or minimum earnings
    are guaranteed.

## Items that must be finalized before activation

- Contracting/payor entity and governing law.
- Affiliate identity, age/entity eligibility and prohibited jurisdictions.
- Payout methods, supported currencies, fees and failed-payment handling.
- Tax forms, withholding and cross-border documentation required by the payor.
- Notice, suspension, appeal, termination and final-payout timelines.
- Privacy notice, retention periods and data-subject request channel.
- Exact attribution precedence if promotion codes or cookies are later added.
