Why pyobfus

Protection is useful only when the resulting software can still be shipped, checked and supported.

A complete free edition

Community handles real multi-file projects with no file or line cap and no trial clock. Optional project limits are your CI safety rails, not an upgrade gate.

Protection that stays diagnosable

Obfuscation normally makes production reports useless: a customer sends you a stack trace full of I0 and I7. pyobfus keeps a mapping, so --unmap turns that trace back into your original names. The mapping stays with you; the customer only ever gets the obfuscated build.

Evidence before and after a build

Preview selection and artifacts, scan compatibility risks, verify generated syntax, and retain reproducible build reports and provenance instead of trusting an opaque one-way command.

Portable, local-first delivery

Your source stays local and Community emits ordinary cross-platform .py. Runtime-backed Pro artifacts ship the separate pure-Python pyobfus-runtime, never the proprietary builder or a native build matrix.

Claims you can inspect

Threat-model limits, advisory-only compatibility and non-cryptographic deterrents are labelled honestly. Tested claims link to evidence rather than being presented as guarantees.

Start in two commands

Nothing to sign up for.

# Free, no limits on files or lines
pip install pyobfus
pyobfus src/ -o dist/ --save-mapping mapping.json

# Later, when a customer sends you an obfuscated traceback
pyobfus --unmap trace.txt --mapping mapping.json
